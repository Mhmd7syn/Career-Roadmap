# Career & Life Master Graph

> **Core Philosophy:** All skills, domains, and career assets are organized as an organic graph. Knowledge is anchored in the file system for total ownership and lifelong persistence.

---

## Domains

1. **Tech Domain (`tech`):** Implements the **Tri-T Engineer** model across parallel tracks:
   - **Track 0: Core CS Foundations** (Algorithms, Systems, Networking)
   - **Track 1: Software Engineering (SWE)** (APIs, DBs, Docker, System Design)
   - **Track 2: Data Ecosystem** (SQL Analytics, dbt Warehouses, Airflow, Spark)
   - **Track 3: AI Landscape** (ML, PyTorch, Transformers, RAG, MLOps)
   - **Milestone Fusion Projects** (Continuous cross-domain synthesis)

2. **Freelancing & Consulting (`freelancing`):** Monetization, scoping, contracts, client delivery.
3. **Marketing & Personal Brand (`marketing`):** Technical writing, open-source presence, inbound lead generation.

---

## Navigation & Mechanics

- **BFS Traversal:** Always survey the macro breadth across tracks at Depth $d=1$ before drilling down to Depth $d=2$ and beyond.
- **Station Documents:** Each station maintains `info.md` (overview and mental models) and `notes.md` (study notes and code).
