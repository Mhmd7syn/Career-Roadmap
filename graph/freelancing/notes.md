# Freelancing & High-Value Consulting

> **Domain Scope:** Direct commercialization of engineering skills through consulting, contract architecture, and freelance execution.

### Key Milestones
- **Positioning:** Transitioning from generic developer to specialized systems architect.
- **Value Pricing:** Decoupling compensation from hours worked ($Value = \Delta \text{Revenue} + \Delta \text{Cost Saved}$).
- **Client Pipeline:** Direct outreach, inbound technical authority, and repeat retainer contracts.
