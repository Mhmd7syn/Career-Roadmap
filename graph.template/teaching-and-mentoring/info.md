# Teaching & Mentoring

**Status:** todo

Pedagogy, technical mentorship, knowledge transfer, active coaching, community building, and engineering leadership.

## Learning Outcomes
- Apply structured pedagogical frameworks (Feynman technique, Bloom's taxonomy, progressive disclosure).
- Conduct impactful 1-on-1 mentorship sessions that diagnose mental blocks and accelerate mentee growth.
- Design structured technical curricula, hands-on lab exercises, and self-guided roadmaps.
- Provide empathetic, actionable code reviews and strategic career guidance.
- Build and nurture collaborative learning communities, study circles, and technical knowledge-sharing platforms.
