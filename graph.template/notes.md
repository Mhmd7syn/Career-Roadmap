# Knowledge Graph

## Type of thinking

- **Focus:** Concentrate
  - Step-by-step reasoning, calculations
  - Solving complex problems
  - Use familiar neural pathways
- **Diffuse:** when relaxed
  - Broaden thinking or connections between ideas
  - Get new ideas or understand new thinking

## Procrastination

- gives unhappy feelings
- push to do other tasks to get temporary happiness
- Solution: Pomodoro (25 min work + 2-5 min Retrive + 3 min rest)

## Memory Types

- **Working Memory**
- **Long-term Memory:**
  - Practice and repeat
  - Use spaced repetition

## Sleep

- The brain erases unimportant info and strengthens the important ones

## Notes

- Metaphor and analogy are important for learning new things
- We need to mix both modes by concentrating, then relaxing and repeating
- Learning Math or theories is hard to understand without real-life examples and relations
- Some of your best ideas appear when you stop forcing them (diffuse thinking)
- Focus remains valuable; multitasking is often just efficient switching.
- Leave the difficult task and return to it later, as the brain will continue parallel processing
