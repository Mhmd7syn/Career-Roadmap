# Sales

**Status:** todo

Revenue generation, outbound prospecting, B2B sales cycles, consultative qualification, objection handling, and deal closing.

## Learning Outcomes
- Master consultative and solution selling frameworks (MEDDPICC, BANT, SPIN Selling).
- Build repeatable outbound prospecting funnels across cold outreach, LinkedIn, and warm networks.
- Conduct structured discovery calls that uncover urgent customer pain and quantify financial impact.
- Handle objections around pricing, timing, and authority, and negotiate win-win contracts.
- Manage structured sales pipelines, deal stages, and CRM hygiene.
