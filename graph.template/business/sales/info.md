# Sales

**Status:** todo

Foundations of consultative sales: diagnosing customer bottlenecks, baseline qualification frameworks, and articulating commercial value.

## Learning Outcomes
- Internalize the consultative selling principle: diagnose client pain before recommending solutions.
- Apply the BANT framework (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) to qualify early commercial conversations.
- Draft a concise, non-spammy outreach message tailored to a prospective client's known problem.
- Lead a structured 15-minute introductory call to isolate priorities and gauge commercial fit.
