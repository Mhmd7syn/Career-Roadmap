- Write high-signal technical case studies detailing architecture trade-offs, benchmarks, and post-mortems.
- Maintain open-source repositories with clean documentation, reproducible READMEs, and quickstart guides.
- Build inbound visibility across technical platforms (GitHub, LinkedIn, technical blogs, Substack).
- Present complex engineering concepts clearly to both technical peers and non-technical stakeholders.
