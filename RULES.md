# Roadmap & Content Rules

## 1. Starter Node Scoping
- **Launchpad Only:** Station `info.md` files provide only foundational orientation, mental models, and initial setup—never advanced mastery of child topics.
- **Delegate Depth:** Advanced specializations, deep tooling, and detailed implementations belong strictly in child subfolders.

## 2. High-Yield Sourcing (3–4 Links Cap)
- **Hard Cap:** Strictly 3 to 4 links per station (e.g., 1 practice/sandbox, 1 quickstart/cheatsheet, 1 focused guide). Avoid encyclopedic dumps.
- **Practice First:** Prioritize interactive platforms, cheatsheets, and active recall drills.

## 3. Formatting & Content Separation
- **Minimal & Direct:** Zero filler text, verbose prose, or decorative emojis. Keep `info.md` concise.
- **Document Separation:** `info.md` is strictly for starter orientation and curated links; personal notes, code, and derivations belong in `notes.md`.

## 4. The 3-Pass Learning Engine
1. **Pass 1 (Concept Intake):** Quick intake of core fundamentals via short guides or cheatsheets.
2. **Pass 2 (Active Recall):** Rapid self-testing and drills from memory.
3. **Pass 3 (Production Synthesis):** One working project, reproducible lab, or code artifact before completing the station.
