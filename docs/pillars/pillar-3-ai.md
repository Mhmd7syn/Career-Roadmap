# Pillar 3: Artificial Intelligence (AI)

## Breadth Survey Scope
- **Classical ML:** Scikit-Learn, regression, classification, tree ensembles (XGBoost/LightGBM), metrics (ROC-AUC, F1).
- **Deep Learning:** PyTorch tensors, autograd, forward/backward loops, standard neural architectures.
- **CV & NLP:** CNNs, ViTs, tokenization, embeddings, attention mechanism, Transformers (BERT/GPT).
- **GenAI & LLMs:** Prompt engineering, structured outputs, RAG architectures, Vector DBs (pgvector/Qdrant), Agent loops.
- **Adaptation:** Fine-tuning basics (PEFT, LoRA, QLoRA).
- **Serving & MLOps:** vLLM, Ollama, quantization, MLflow, Langfuse tracing, Ragas/DeepEval metrics.
- **Reinforcement Learning:** Fundamentals, RLHF overview.

## Candidate Deep Dives
- LLM application systems & multi-agent architectures.
- Fine-tuning & high-throughput inference serving.
- Multimodal / Vision AI systems.
